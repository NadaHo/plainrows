Google Trends Scraper & Google Trends API: Regions & Queries

Google Trends scraper, API and MCP tool: interest over time for up to 5 compared keywords, interest by region, related queries and topics (top and rising). Any country, region or worldwide; web, YouTube, news, images and shopping search. No proxies. Pay per search (fast mode costs extra).

Try it on Apify  

Typical uses

  • Keyword and content research: is a topic growing or fading, when does it peak each year, which related queries are rising right now.
  • Brand and competitor comparison: compare up to 5 brands or products on one 0-100 scale, by week and by region.
  • Seasonality for e-commerce and ads: weekly or monthly demand curves to plan stock, campaigns and budgets.
  • Market and location research: where in a country a product or service is searched the most, region by region.
  • Dashboards, automations and AI agents: feed Google Sheets, BigQuery, n8n, Make, Zapier or an MCP client with flat JSON rows that chart directly.

Ready-to-run examples

  • Compare ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude on Google Trends: Compare the Google search interest of ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude over the past 12 months, worldwide, with one row per week and keyword plus interest by country.
  • Rising and top related queries for a keyword: Get Google Trends related queries and related topics (top and rising) for a keyword, with interest over time: find breakout searches for content and SEO ideas.
  • YouTube search trends: compare games on YouTube: Measure YouTube search interest instead of web search, as in the Google Trends YouTube menu: Minecraft vs Roblox vs Fortnite over 12 months, worldwide, with interest by country.
  • Google Shopping trends: compare products over 5 years: Compare Google Shopping search interest for kitchen products in the United Kingdom over 5 years, to time stock and promotions on seasonal peaks.

Run it from your code

Get an API token in the Apify Console (Settings → Integrations), then:

curl -X POST \
  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/plainrows~google-trends-explorer/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
  "searchTerms": [
    "chatgpt, gemini, claude",
    "coffee"
  ],
  "country": "US",
  "timeRange": "past_12_months"
}'
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("plainrows/google-trends-explorer").call(run_input={'searchTerms': ['chatgpt, gemini, claude', 'coffee'],
 'country': 'US',
 'timeRange': 'past_12_months'})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(row)
