Find local businesses without a website

You sell websites, local SEO or Google Business Profile work. Your best prospects are businesses that customers find on Google Maps but that have no website to send them to. Here is how to get that list for any trade and city, with a phone number to call.

The list in one run

In Google Maps Businesses Scraper, type the searches you would type in Google Maps, one per line, and set two filters:

  • searchQueries: plumber in Leeds, hair salon in Leeds, restaurant in Leeds… (up to about 700 places per search; split a big city into districts or postcodes to go further);
  • website: without (keeps only places that list no website);
  • requirePhone: true (drops places you could not call).

Each row gives the business name, category, address, phone, rating, number of reviews, opening hours, GPS coordinates, Place ID and Google Maps link. Download it as CSV or Excel, or send it to Google Sheets or your CRM.

What it costs

$0.0015 per place delivered (Apify Starter plan). Places removed by the website, requirePhone or minRating filters are not billed: you pay for the leads you keep, not for the businesses that already have a site. Each run stops at the maximum cost you set.

For comparison, the most used Google Maps scraper on Apify (compass/crawler-google-places) charges $0.003 per place on the Starter plan plus a "Filter applied" add-on of $0.001 per place (prices read on 2026-10-11).

Make the list better

  • Sort by number of reviews: a business with 80 reviews and no website gets traffic it cannot convert, an easy pitch.
  • Low rating and no website: pitch reputation management together with the site.
  • isClaimed is false: the owner never claimed the Google listing, so offer the Google Business Profile setup as well.
  • Run the same searches every month: new businesses appear on Google Maps before they have a website.

Good to know

  • "No website" means no website on the Google Maps listing. Some of these businesses have a Facebook page or a site they never added: check before you call.
  • The data comes from Google Maps listings through a commercial data API: no browser, no proxies, results in seconds.
  • Calling or e-mailing businesses is subject to the rules of your country (for example GDPR and PECR in the UK and EU, TCPA in the US).
