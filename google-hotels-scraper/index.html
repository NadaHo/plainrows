Google Hotels Scraper: Prices, Ratings & Booking Sites

Google Hotels scraper and MCP tool for any city and dates: hotel name, nightly price, stay total, stars, rating, reviews, GPS, link, 140 hotels per search, or every booking site's price for given hotels with a daily calendar. No browser or proxies. Pay per hotel plus a small fee per results page.

Try it on Apify  

Typical uses

  • Hotel price tracking: schedule a daily run on your city and dates and follow the price of each hotel (`hotelId` is stable).
  • Revenue management and rate shopping: see where your hotel's price sits against every competitor of the same class.
  • Travel apps and AI agents: give an assistant live hotel prices for "3 nights in Lisbon next month for 2 adults".
  • Market research and price indexes: average nightly price by city, star class or date, cheapest hotel per city (`sortBy: lowest_price`).
  • Automations: send the results to Google Sheets, Airtable, a database or Slack with n8n, Make or Zapier.

Ready-to-run examples

  • 5-star hotels in London with a spa, by price: List 5-star London hotels that have a spa, cheapest first, with nightly price, total price, rating and reviews. Change the city, stars or amenities to build any hotel shortlist.

Run it from your code

Get an API token in the Apify Console (Settings → Integrations), then:

curl -X POST \
  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/plainrows~google-hotels-scraper/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
  "destinations": [
    "Paris, France"
  ],
  "currency": "USD",
  "country": "US"
}'
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("plainrows/google-hotels-scraper").call(run_input={'destinations': ['Paris, France'], 'currency': 'USD', 'country': 'US'})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(row)
