Which of your keywords show a Google AI Overview?

If a keyword shows an AI Overview, fewer people click the organic results. Before you plan content or report on lost traffic, you need to know which keywords on your list have one. Checking them one by one in Google is slow. Scraping a live results page for every keyword costs a search each. Here is a bulk check that comes in the same row as the search volume.

One row per keyword, SERP features included

Paste up to 10,000 keywords in Keyword Search Volume, CPC & Difficulty and choose one of 94 countries. Each keyword comes back with:

  • serpFeatures: the result types Google showed for that keyword, for example ai_overview, organic, people_also_ask, popular_products, video, local_pack;
  • serpUpdatedAt: the date of that results-page snapshot, so you can tell recent snapshots from old ones;
  • monthly search volume, CPC, competition, keyword difficulty, search intent and 12 months of history.

Filter on serpFeatures contains ai_overview, sort by search volume, and you have the list of keywords where an AI Overview sits above your result.

What we saw on 760 US keywords (2026-10-13)

We ran 760 product keywords such as "best air fryer", "standing desk review" and "yoga mat for beginners". 566 had a results-page snapshot. 253 of them (45 %) showed an AI Overview. AI Overviews were more common on informational keywords (65 %) and commercial ones like "best …" and "… review" (60 %) than on transactional ones like "… for sale" and "… deals" (30 %). Snapshot dates varied: 269 were from July to October 2026, and the rest were older.

Need the AI answer itself?

This check tells you whether an AI Overview appears. If you want to know what the AI answers say and which sites they cite or mention, use AI Search Brand Visibility. It asks ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode your questions and reports whether your brand and your domain appear.

Good to know

  • SERP features come from the latest snapshot in a keyword database, not from a live search made during your run. Check serpUpdatedAt. For a live check of a few keywords, a SERP scraper is the right tool.
  • AI Overviews change often for the same keyword. Treat a single snapshot as "this keyword can show an AI Overview", not as a guarantee.
  • Some keywords have volume data but no results-page snapshot. For those, serpFeatures is empty.
