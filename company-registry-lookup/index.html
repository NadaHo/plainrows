Company Registry Lookup & API: SIRENE, LEI, Brreg & KYB Data

Company registry lookup and MCP tool: find companies in official registers by name, registration number, VAT number or LEI: France SIRENE, Norway Brreg, Finland PRH, Czech ARES and global LEI. Legal form, status, address, industry, employees, LEI. No personal data. Pay only per company found.

Try it on Apify  

Typical uses

  • KYB and onboarding checks: confirm that a supplier or customer exists, is active, and matches the name, address and registration number it gave you.
  • CRM and lead enrichment: turn a list of SIRENs, org numbers or VAT numbers into legal names, addresses, industry codes, size bands and websites.
  • LEI lookup: find the LEI of a company from its national number, or the registry data behind an LEI.
  • Data cleaning: normalize company names and numbers across France, the Nordics and Czechia, with a common NACE code for cross-country analysis.
  • AI agents: give an agent a reliable "who is this company?" tool backed by official sources.

Ready-to-run examples

  • Look up companies from EU VAT numbers (FR, NO, FI, CZ): Turn VAT numbers into official company records: legal name, registration number, address, legal form, status, NACE code and LEI, from the French, Norwegian, Finnish and Czech registers.
  • French company data from a SIREN number: Get the official SIRENE record of French companies from their SIREN: legal name, head office address, legal form, NAF/NACE code, size band, status and LEI.
  • Find a company's registration number and LEI by name: Search official registers by company name and get the registration number, VAT number, address, status and LEI of the best match: KYB checks and CRM enrichment.
  • LEI lookup: registry data behind a Legal Entity Identifier: Get the GLEIF record behind an LEI: legal name, country, national registration number, legal form, addresses and LEI status.

Run it from your code

Get an API token in the Apify Console (Settings → Integrations), then:

curl -X POST \
  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/plainrows~company-registry-lookup/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
  "queries": [
    "552032534",
    "923609016",
    "0112038-9",
    "00177041",
    "Danone"
  ],
  "country": "auto"
}'
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("plainrows/company-registry-lookup").call(run_input={'queries': ['552032534', '923609016', '0112038-9', '00177041', 'Danone'],
 'country': 'auto'})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(row)
