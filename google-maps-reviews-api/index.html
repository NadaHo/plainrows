Google Maps reviews API: all the reviews, not just 5

You want the reviews of your locations, your competitors or a list of leads, for sentiment analysis, a review dashboard or to spot unanswered complaints. Google's own Places API stops at 5 reviews per place.

What you get per review

Give place IDs, CIDs, Google Maps links or "business name, city" to Google Maps Reviews. Each review is one row: star rating, text (and the original text when Google translated it), date, the owner's reply and its date, photos, review ID and link, plus the place's name, address, average rating and total number of reviews. Reviewer names and profile links are not collected. Places found with Google Maps Scraper can be passed as they are (their placeId column).

Real example, checked on 2026-10-11: the 100 newest reviews of a New York pizzeria with 27,235 reviews, ready in about 20 seconds; 55 of them had a text, the others were star ratings only.

Other ways to get the same data

  • Google Places API (New): the reviews field of a place lists "a maximum of 5 reviews", sorted by relevance (Place reference). Asking for it bills the Place Details Enterprise + Atmosphere SKU: $25 per 1,000 calls after 1,000 free calls a month (pricing page, updated 2026-10-08). Best if 5 reviews per place are enough and you already use Google Maps Platform.
  • Google Business Profile API: lists the reviews of the locations of your own Business Profile account (accounts/{accountId}/locations/{locationId}/reviews, review data guide), once Google has approved your access request, sent from an owner or manager e-mail of the profile (prerequisites). Best for your own locations, and the way to reply to reviews.
