Public tender alerts for your sector, from the EU and the UK in one feed

You sell to public buyers: IT services, cleaning, construction, consulting. New calls for tenders appear every day on TED for the EU and on two UK platforms, each with its own search and e-mail alerts. Here is how to get them in one daily list, filtered for your sector and still open for bids.

Set it up once

In Public Tenders Search:

  • cpvCodes: the CPV codes of your trade (for example 72000000 for IT services, 90910000 for cleaning), or keywords;
  • countries: the buyer countries you can serve;
  • noticeTypes: contract notices (open tenders), and prior information notices if you want to see projects months before the tender;
  • openForBidsOnly: drop notices whose deadline has passed; minValueEur to skip small contracts;
  • onlyNewNotices with an Apify schedule (daily): each run returns only notices not delivered before, so you get and pay for each one once.

Each notice is one row: title, buyer and country, CPV codes with English labels, estimated value (also converted to EUR), deadline, procedure, number of lots, documents link and the official notice page. A notice published on both UK platforms comes once. Send it to Google Sheets, Slack or your CRM with n8n, Make or Zapier.

What it costs

$0.0025 per notice returned (Apify Starter plan), plus a start fee per run (see the Actor's Pricing tab). A daily alert that finds 10 new notices costs about $0.025 a day before the start fee. Each run stops at the maximum cost you set.

Also useful

  • Contract awards list the winners and values: who wins in your market, and which companies just got funded work (subcontracting and supplier leads). Look them up in official registers (France, Norway, Finland, Czechia and the worldwide LEI index) with Company Registry Lookup.

Good to know

  • TED, Find a Tender and Contracts Finder each offer free e-mail alerts on their own site. This tool is for one combined, filtered feed in your own tools, with data you can sort and analyse.
  • Data comes from the official open APIs of the three platforms. Always read the official notice before bidding.
