You want news articles about a brand, a company or a topic inside a script, a spreadsheet, n8n or an AI agent: headline, link, publisher and publication time. Here is where NewsAPI stands, and what PlainRows offers.

NewsAPI plans and their limits

From newsapi.org/pricing on 2026-10-08:

  • Developer (free): 100 requests per day, "Articles have a 24 hour delay", search up to a month old, and it "cannot be used in a staging or production environment".
  • Business: $449 per month for 250,000 requests, real-time articles, search up to 5 years old.
  • Advanced: $1,749 per month for 2,000,000 requests.

Google News itself has no public API.

When NewsAPI is the better choice

  • You are prototyping and a 24-hour delay is fine: the free Developer plan costs nothing.
  • You need an archive of up to 5 years, or several hundred thousand requests a month at a fixed price.
  • You need article content or author fields. PlainRows returns what Google News shows: headline, publisher, snippet and time.

When PlainRows is the better choice

  • You need current articles in production and your volume is far below what $449 a month buys.
  • You want the articles Google News ranks for a query, in a given country and language, with their position and Top stories.
  • You run it from n8n, Make, Zapier, Google Sheets or an AI agent (MCP), with a hard cap: every run stops at the maximum cost you set.
