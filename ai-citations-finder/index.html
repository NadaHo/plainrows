AI Citations Finder: LLM Mentions in ChatGPT & AI Overviews

AI citations finder and MCP tool: real LLM mentions of your domain or brand in ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, no prompts needed. One row per AI question: AI search volume, answer excerpt, cited sources, your source position. Fee per target searched plus per question.

Try it on Apify  

Typical uses

  • AI visibility / GEO audit: which questions make ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews talk about your brand, and how often they are asked.
  • Citation tracking: `targetCitedAsSource` and `targetSourcePosition` tell you if the answer links to your site, and where in the list of sources.
  • Competitor research in AI answers: run it on a competitor's domain, or read `otherCitedDomains` to see who is cited next to you.
  • Content ideas: the questions with a high `aiSearchVolume` where you are mentioned but not cited are pages worth writing.
  • Reports: send the rows to Google Sheets, Looker Studio, Slack, n8n, Make or Zapier.

Run it from your code

Get an API token in the Apify Console (Settings → Integrations), then:

curl -X POST \
  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/plainrows~ai-citations-finder/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
  "targets": [
    "pipedrive.com"
  ],
  "platform": "google",
  "country": "US",
  "maxQuestionsPerTarget": 10
}'
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("plainrows/ai-citations-finder").call(run_input={'targets': ['pipedrive.com'],
 'platform': 'google',
 'country': 'US',
 'maxQuestionsPerTarget': 10})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(row)
