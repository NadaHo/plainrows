Google Maps leads with e-mails: what one lead costs

You want local businesses from Google Maps with a way to reach them: phone, website, and the e-mail and social profiles published on their website. Several Apify tools do it; here is what each charges per place, and what PlainRows offers.

Popular Google Maps scrapers with contact enrichment

Prices on the Apify Starter plan, read from each tool's pricing on 2026-10-13:

  • Google Maps Scraper (compass/crawler-google-places): $0.003 per place, plus $0.002 per place for "Company contacts enrichment", plus $0.001 per place for each filter applied. About $0.005 per place with contacts.
  • Google Maps Email Extractor (lukaskrivka/google-maps-with-contact-details): $0.004 per place, plus $0.002 per place for contacts enrichment, plus $0.001 per place for each filter. About $0.006 per place with contacts.

In both, the contacts add-on is charged per place processed.

When another tool is the better choice

  • You need reviews, photos, popular times or the names and job titles of employees: the compass scraper has add-ons for these.
  • You need e-mails that only appear after JavaScript runs or deep inside a site. PlainRows reads the homepage and up to two contact or legal pages, without a browser.
  • You need more than 700 places for one search.

When PlainRows is the better choice

  • You build prospect lists and want to pay for contacts only when there are some.
  • You want results in seconds from a commercial data API, with free filters.
  • You run it from n8n, Make, Zapier, Google Sheets or an AI agent (MCP), with a hard cap: every run stops at the maximum cost you set.
