Is your brand in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode answers?

More and more buyers ask an AI assistant "what is the best CRM for a small agency?" instead of searching. You want to know, question by question, whether the answer names you, links your site, and which competitors it names instead.

What a check returns

AI Search Visibility Tracker asks each of your questions to ChatGPT (with web search), Gemini and Google AI Mode, in the country and language you choose, and returns one row per answer:

  • brandMentioned and brandMentionRank: are you named, and before or after your competitors;
  • brandDomainCited and brandDomainCitationRank: is your site among the sources or links of the answer;
  • competitorsMentioned, competitorDomainsCited, citedDomains and sources: who else is named, and which pages the answer relies on;
  • fanOutQueries: the web searches ChatGPT ran to answer, and optionally the full answer text.

Real check on 2026-10-09 ("best project management tool for a small agency", brand Asana): named by all three engines, first in Google AI Mode, second after ClickUp in ChatGPT and after Monday.com in Gemini; asana.com cited by ChatGPT as its 9th link, not by the other two.

Other ways to do it

  • Ask the assistants yourself: fine for five questions, slow for fifty, and your own account's history can colour the answers.
  • Plain answer scrapers return the text and sources (Apify's ChatGPT Search Scraper costs $0.005 per answer on the Starter plan on 2026-10-09); you then check the brand and competitors yourself.
  • AI visibility platforms add dashboards and alerts, usually as a monthly subscription.

AI answers vary from one run to the next: schedule the check weekly and follow the trend rather than a single answer.
