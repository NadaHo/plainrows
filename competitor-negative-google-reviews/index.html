Your competitors' 1-star reviews, all in one sheet

The fastest market research for a local business or an agency: what customers complain about at every competitor nearby. Late deliveries, rude staff, hidden fees: each complaint is a promise you can make in your ads and on your site. Here is how to collect them for a whole area in one run.

Step 1: list the competitors

Run Google Maps Businesses Scraper with a search like dentist in Leeds: it returns every place with its place ID, rating and number of reviews. Keep the ones you compete with.

Step 2: their worst reviews first

Paste their place IDs (or "business name, city", or Google Maps links) in Google Maps Reviews with sortBy set to lowest_rating and, for example, 50 reviews per place. Each review is one row: stars, text (and the original text when Google translated it), date, the owner's reply and its date, plus the place's name, address and average rating. Reviewer names and profiles are not collected.

Then group the texts by theme in a spreadsheet, or give them to an LLM with a prompt like "list the 10 most frequent complaints with one quote each".

What it costs

$0.00027 per review delivered plus $0.0011 per block of 10 reviews requested (Apify Starter plan). 20 competitors with 50 reviews each: about $0.27 for the reviews plus $0.11 for the blocks. Each run stops at the maximum cost you set.

Keep watching

Turn on onlyNewReviews and schedule a weekly run with sortBy: newest: you get only the reviews posted since the last run, for your own locations and your competitors'.

Good to know

  • Use what you learn to improve your offer and your messages; quoting or naming competitors' customers in public is another matter.
  • Review texts are delivered as written: they may themselves mention names.
