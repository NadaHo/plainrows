Google News API to monitor brands and topics: title, URL, publisher, snippet, publication time and position for every article, in 20 countries. Time filter, sort by date, only-new-articles mode for scheduled alerts (no duplicates). A NewsAPI and Google Alerts alternative. Pay per article found.

Typical uses

  • Brand and competitor monitoring: turn on Only new articles, schedule a daily run on your brand names and get (and pay for) each new article mentioning them once.
  • PR and media relations: see which outlets cover a topic, how high they rank and when they published.
  • Market and investment watch: follow companies, tickers, products or regulations in several countries at once.
  • Content and SEO teams: find what is trending in Google News for your keywords, with the publishers that rank.
  • Automations and AI agents: feed n8n, Make, Zapier, Slack, Google Sheets or an MCP client with structured JSON.

Ready-to-run examples

  • Monitor brand mentions in Google News (last 24h): Get every Google News article mentioning a brand in the last 24 hours, newest first, with publisher, snippet and date. Schedule it daily.
  • Collect this week's news on an industry topic: Collect Google News articles from the past week on a topic, with publisher, URL, snippet and publication date.
  • Get only new Google News articles about your brand: Schedule this task daily: the first run lists today's articles, each later run returns only articles it has never delivered, so you pay once per article. Send them to Slack, e-mail or a sheet.

Run it from your code

Get an API token in the Apify Console (Settings → Integrations), then:

curl -X POST \
  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/plainrows~google-news-scraper/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
  "queries": [
    "Tesla",
    "\"electric vehicles\" Europe"
  ],
  "country": "US"
}'
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("plainrows/google-news-scraper").call(run_input={'queries': ['Tesla', '"electric vehicles" Europe'], 'country': 'US'})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(row)
