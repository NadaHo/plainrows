Google Maps Rank Tracker: Geo-Grid Local SEO

See where a business ranks on Google Maps at every point of a grid around it (3x3 to 9x9) for up to 5 keywords: rank per point, top 3 competitors, average rank and share of local voice. Pay per grid point, no subscription.

Typical uses

  • Local SEO agencies: monthly geo-grid reports for each client, at cost per point, scheduled and sent to a spreadsheet or a dashboard.
  • Business owners: see the streets where you disappear from the map pack, and who takes your place there.
  • Before / after: measure the effect of a Google Business Profile change, new reviews or a new category on the same grid.
  • Competitor research: the competitor ranked first at the most points, with its rating and review count.
  • AI agents (MCP): "how visible is this dentist in its neighbourhood?" answered with numbers.

Run it from your code

Get an API token in the Apify Console (Settings → Integrations), then:

curl -X POST \
  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/plainrows~google-maps-rank-tracker/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
  "business": "Monmouth Coffee Company, Borough, London",
  "keywords": [
    "coffee shop"
  ],
  "gridSize": "5",
  "country": "GB"
}'
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("plainrows/google-maps-rank-tracker").call(run_input={'business': 'Monmouth Coffee Company, Borough, London',
 'keywords': ['coffee shop'],
 'gridSize': '5',
 'country': 'GB'})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(row)
