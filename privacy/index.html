Contact & Privacy

PlainRows publishes data tools (Actors) on the Apify Store. They run on Apify's platform, in the account of the person who starts them.

Contact

Open an issue in the Issues tab of any PlainRows Actor on the Apify Store (apify.com/plainrows). Questions, bug reports, removal and opt-out requests all go there. This is the only contact channel for now.

What data the Actors process

  • Only publicly available data: search results, public business listings, public web pages and similar sources.
  • Contact-details options, where an Actor offers them, are limited to business contacts published on a company's own website. They exclude personal social profiles (such as LinkedIn /in/ pages) and team pages, and skip websites that state an explicit objection to unsolicited contact.
  • Results are stored in the Apify account of the user who ran the Actor, not by PlainRows.

Opt-out and removal requests

If you want your website or your data excluded from PlainRows Actors, or removed from what they return, open an issue as described above with the website or data concerned. Requests are answered within 30 days.

Your responsibility as a user

You decide what you collect with these tools and what you do with it. You are responsible for using the data lawfully, in particular under the GDPR and the ePrivacy rules on direct marketing (for example, consent before sending marketing e-mails where the law requires it).

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service (see GitHub's privacy statement). Actor pages show a status badge image loaded from apify.com.
