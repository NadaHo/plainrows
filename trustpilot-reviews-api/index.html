Trustpilot reviews API: new reviews of any company, billed per review

You want the latest Trustpilot reviews of your company, or of your competitors, in a sheet, a Slack channel or an AI summary. Trustpilot's own business APIs are built for a company managing its own reviews (invitations, replies, widgets), and its database of reviews is sold separately as Data Solutions (Trustpilot developer site).

What you get per review

Paste company domains or Trustpilot links in Trustpilot Reviews Scraper. Each review is one row: stars, title, text, language, date, verified flag and the company's reply, plus the company's name, average rating and total number of reviews. Reviewer names and profiles are not collected.

New reviews only, every day

Turn on onlyNewReviews and schedule the run daily: each run returns only reviews not delivered before, so you can answer unhappy customers fast and pay for each review once.

What it costs

$0.00027 per review delivered, plus $0.0022 per block of 20 reviews requested (Apify Starter plan), plus a start fee per run (see the Actor's Pricing tab). A company with no review still pays the blocks requested (5 for the default 100 reviews); duplicates and malformed inputs are free, and each run stops at the maximum cost you set.

Also on Google and Tripadvisor

Track the same company on Google Maps Reviews and, for hotels and restaurants, Tripadvisor Reviews: the rows have the same shape, so all platforms fit in one sheet.

Good to know

  • Up to 200 of the most recent (or most relevant) reviews per company per run: made for monitoring, not for exporting a full history.
  • Review and reply texts are delivered as written: they may themselves mention names.
  • Blocks of 20 are charged for each company Trustpilot answers, even when it has fewer reviews than requested: ask for 20 or 40 reviews per company for a daily check.
