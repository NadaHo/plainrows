AI Visibility Tracker: ChatGPT Visibility & Google AI Mode

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode the questions your customers ask and see if your brand is mentioned, your site cited, and which competitors show up. Answers, sources and fan-out queries as clean rows. API-based, no browser or accounts. Pay per answer, plus a small fee per question answered.

Try it on Apify  

Typical uses

  • AI search visibility (GEO / AEO): are you recommended when people ask "best CRM for a small agency" or "is Acme reliable?" — and in which position compared with your competitors?
  • Citation tracking: which pages do the engines cite for your topics, and is your site one of them? Spot the reviews and comparison sites you need to be on.
  • Competitor monitoring: who gets named instead of you, engine by engine, country by country.
  • Content planning: the fan-out queries show what ChatGPT searched for to answer; the cited pages show what content wins.
  • Weekly reports and alerts: schedule a run and send the table to Google Sheets, Looker Studio, Slack, n8n, Make or Zapier.

Ready-to-run examples

  • Is your CRM recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Mode?: Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode which CRM a small agency should use, and see whether HubSpot is mentioned, in which position against Pipedrive, Salesforce and Zoho, and whether hubspot.com is cited.
  • Which running shoe brands do AI assistants recommend?: See which running shoe brands ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode recommend for flat feet and for beginners, whether Brooks is named before Asics, Hoka and Nike, and which review sites the answers cite.
  • Which websites do AI answers cite for your topic?: Collect the sources ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode cite when asked about a topic, with the full answers and ChatGPT's web searches: find the review and comparison pages you need to appear on.

Run it from your code

Get an API token in the Apify Console (Settings → Integrations), then:

curl -X POST \
  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/plainrows~ai-search-brand-visibility/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
  "prompts": [
    "best standing desk for small spaces",
    "best electric standing desk under $500"
  ],
  "brandNames": [
    "FlexiSpot"
  ],
  "brandDomain": "flexispot.com",
  "competitors": [
    "Uplift",
    "Desky",
    "IKEA"
  ],
  "country": "US"
}'
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("plainrows/ai-search-brand-visibility").call(run_input={'prompts': ['best standing desk for small spaces',
             'best electric standing desk under $500'],
 'brandNames': ['FlexiSpot'],
 'brandDomain': 'flexispot.com',
 'competitors': ['Uplift', 'Desky', 'IKEA'],
 'country': 'US'})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(row)
