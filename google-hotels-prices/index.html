Google Hotels Prices Scraper: Price Comparison of Booking Sites

Hotel price tracker and MCP tool for Google Hotels: nightly rates by city and dates (stars, rating, GPS, link), or every booking site's price for given hotels (Booking.com, Expedia, official site) for several dates, with a daily price calendar. Pay per hotel plus a small fee per results page.

Try it on Apify  

Typical uses

  • Hotel price tracking: schedule a daily run on your city and dates and follow the price of each hotel (`hotelId` is stable).
  • Revenue management and rate shopping: see where your hotel's price sits against every competitor of the same class.
  • Travel apps and AI agents: give an assistant live hotel prices for "3 nights in Lisbon next month for 2 adults".
  • Market research and price indexes: average nightly price by city, star class or date, cheapest hotel per city (`sortBy: lowest_price`).
  • Automations: send the results to Google Sheets, Airtable, a database or Slack with n8n, Make or Zapier.

Ready-to-run examples

  • Find the cheapest well-rated hotels in Lisbon: Lisbon hotels rated 4+ sorted by lowest price, with price per night and free-cancellation filter.
  • Compare a hotel's price on every booking site: Get the price of a hotel on every booking site Google lists (Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, the hotel's own website...) for your dates, cheapest first: rate parity checks and competitor rate shopping.
  • Find the cheapest nights for a hotel (price calendar): Get a hotel's lowest nightly price for every night Google has (about 9-10 months ahead) and the cheapest check-in date, plus today's price on each booking site.
  • Monitor hotel prices in New York every day: Schedule this task daily: the check-in date stays empty, so each run prices New York hotels 30 days ahead. Track how competitor hotels move their nightly rates, for revenue management or travel deals.

Run it from your code

Get an API token in the Apify Console (Settings → Integrations), then:

curl -X POST \
  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/plainrows~google-hotels-prices/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
  "destinations": [
    "Paris, France"
  ],
  "currency": "USD",
  "country": "US"
}'
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("plainrows/google-hotels-prices").call(run_input={'destinations': ['Paris, France'], 'currency': 'USD', 'country': 'US'})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(row)
