Typical uses

  • Competitor backlink research: see which sites link to a competitor, with which anchor, and which links pass authority (dofollow).
  • Link building prospecting: list the referring domains of competing pages, sorted by rank, and contact the best ones.
  • Backlink gap: list the referring domains of your site and of your competitors, then keep the sites that link to two or three competitors but not to you: the warmest outreach targets ([free n8n template](https://nadaho.github.io/plainrows/templates/backlink-gap-sheets.json)).
  • Backlink audits: spot spammy, broken, lost or nofollow links pointing to your site.
  • New link monitoring: sort by newest to see links found recently; schedule the run weekly.
  • Automations and AI agents: feed n8n, Make, Zapier, Google Sheets or an MCP client with structured JSON.

Ready-to-run examples

  • Backlinks API: backlinks of any domain as JSON: Call the Backlinks API with a domain and get its backlinks as JSON or CSV: source page, anchor text, dofollow, source domain rank, spam score, first and last seen. The same call works from Python, JavaScript, n8n, Make or an AI agent (MCP).
  • Sites that link most to a competitor: The referring domains with the most backlinks to a competitor, with dofollow count and first-seen date: where their links come from.
  • Dofollow backlink checker: new dofollow links of a site: Check the dofollow backlinks of any website, newest first and one per referring domain: source page, anchor text, domain rank, spam score, first seen. Monitor link building or negative SEO.
  • Backlink anchor text checker: anchors of a site's links: See the anchor text of the links pointing to any website, one row per referring domain, strongest first: anchor, dofollow or nofollow, linking page, domain rank and spam score. Spot over-optimised or spammy anchors.
  • Find lost backlinks of a website: List live and recently lost backlinks of a domain (isLost = true), newest first, to win back links that disappeared.

  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/plainrows~backlink-checker/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
  "targets": [
    "backlinko.com"
  ],
  "maxResultsPerTarget": 100
}'
