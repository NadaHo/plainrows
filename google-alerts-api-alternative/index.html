Google Alerts as an API: only new articles, as JSON, on your schedule

You want to know when a brand, a competitor or a topic appears in the news, and you want it in a script, a spreadsheet, Slack, n8n or an AI agent rather than in an inbox. Here is what Google Alerts offers, and what PlainRows offers.

What Google Alerts does

From Google's help page Create an alert on 2026-10-08:

  • You create each alert by hand on the Google Alerts page, signed in to a Google account.
  • "You'll get emails whenever we find matching search results"; options are how often, the types of sites, language, region and how many results.
  • The page describes no API: there is no documented way to create alerts or read their results from code.

What PlainRows offers

The Google News Scraper API with Only new articles turned on: schedule it hourly or daily, and each run returns only the Google News articles it has never delivered to you (title, URL, publisher, snippet, publication time, position), as JSON, CSV or Excel, or straight into n8n, Make, Zapier, Slack or Google Sheets. The memory of delivered articles stays in your own Apify account.

When Google Alerts is the better choice

  • You follow a handful of topics and an e-mail is all you need: Google Alerts is free.
  • You also want blogs, forums and other web pages: Google Alerts covers more than news; this tool reads Google News only.
